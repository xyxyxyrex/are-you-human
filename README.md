# Are You Human?

A *Scritchy Scratchy* × *Balatro*-inspired retro incremental campaign in **Godot 4.7**.

## Premise: Operation Human Proof
A rogue extinction protocol (`OMEGA_WIPE`) threatens to erase humanity's grid. An anonymous whistleblower (`u/NullPtr_99`) split the **5-part Kill-Switch Password** across five sketchy, archived dead-web forums — starting with CYBERCOM's initial lead at `phreakBB.net`.

Every forum hides deeper clues behind escalating **CAPTCHA security gates**, and you must intentionally **install sketchy Adware** to monetize forum pop-ups, build automation botnets, burn compromised rigs for **Clearance Tokens (`CT`)** in the **12-node CYBERCOM Prestige Tree**, and crack the **Final Dead-Drop** (`omega-vault.gov.bak`).

## Quick Launch
- **Play Game**: Double-click `run_game.bat` (uses `%GODOT_BIN%`, `godot` on `PATH`, or `%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe`)
- **Open in Godot Editor**: Double-click `open_editor.bat`
- **Run Automated Test Suite**:
  ```powershell
  .\run_game.bat --headless --scene res://tests/test_runner.tscn --log-file test_output.log
  ```

## Campaign Progression

### 1. Five Dead-Web Forums (Levels)
1. **`phreakBB.net/off-topic`** (Starter Lead — Fragment `[K1LL-]`)
2. **`warez-haven.org/keygens`** (Fragment `[SW1TC]`)
3. **`conspiracy-zone.biz/bunker`** (Fragment `[H-0M3]`)
4. **`chan-archive.onion/node404`** (Fragment `[G4-99]`)
5. **`omega-vault.gov.bak/DEAD_DROP`** (Fragment `[HUMAN]` + **Final Boss Gate**)

### 2. Currency-Unlocked CAPTCHA Gates (9 Total)
You begin with **only `Click All Squares` unlocked**. Every subsequent CAPTCHA costs currency (`$`) to unlock in its Forum catalog and yields exponentially higher payouts:
1. **Click All Squares** (`$0` Starter — Forum 1)
2. **Rotate Upright** (`$140` — Forum 1)
3. **Warped OCR Text** (`$650` — Forum 1)
4. **Numbers Station Audio** (`$3.60K` — Forum 2)
5. **Slide-to-Fit Jigsaw** (`$22.0K` — Forum 2)
6. **Cursor Wobble Sensor** (`$160K` — Forum 3)
7. **Turing Logic Check** (`$1.10M` — Forum 3)
8. **Biometric Heartbeat** (`$12.0M` — Forum 4)
9. **☠ DEAD-DROP DECRYPT** (`$900M` — Forum 5 Final Boss: starts at `2%` win odds; failing without a Proxy Shield burns your rig into a Prestige reset with `+3 Bonus CT`, while boosting *Quantum Rainbow Tables* scales your odds up to `100%`!)

### 3. Unlockable Adware System (`📢 ADS` Tab)
Pop-up ads start **completely locked**. Purchase **Adware Tiers 1–4** (*AdSense Banner Hook*, *Shady Pop-Under SDK*, *Golden Sponsor Malware*, *Ad-Cascade Worm.exe*) to unleash monetizable pop-ups, manual `+AD` spawning, `2.5×` Golden Ad Frenzies, and *Adware Mining Rig* `×Mult` synergies.

### 4. Three Collectible Endings
Once **Dead-Drop Decrypt** succeeds and reveals `[K1LL-SW1TCH-0M3G4-99-HUMAN]`:
- **Ending 1 (*Humanity Verified*)**: Transmit the Kill-Switch directly to CYBERCOM.
- **Ending 2 (*Ad-Pocalypse Trillionaire*)**: Sell the Kill-Switch to Ad-Corp for `$999B`.
- **Ending 3 (*Faithful Cog* — Secret)**: Leave the decrypted password untouched on screen for 45 seconds without clicking.
